A coil fed from the mains, buzzing against the object: hums, drones and electric buzz.

**Try it:** load **Drones → Smokestack Hum**, hold a note and turn **Voltage Sag** up.

## What it is

An electromagnet near the object pulls on it at the mains frequency. The object rings at its own pitch while the hum drives it at the mains rate, and an unstable supply adds harmonics of the hum.

## How it sounds

Steady, electric and droning: transformers, amplifiers, machine rooms.

## Controls

<ModelControls exciter="Electromagnetic Hum" />

- **Mains Frequency:** the frequency of the drive, from <Data param="mains_frequency" field="range" /> Hz. It sets the hum's base pitch.
- **Coil Proximity:** how close the coil sits to the object. Higher couples more strongly and sounds louder; at its minimum the hum doesn't reach the object.
- **Voltage Sag:** the instability of the supply. Higher adds buzzing harmonics to the hum.

Neither **Speed** nor **Roughness** affects it.

## Pairings

On **Pipe** or **Industrial Cog** it gives a droning hum; on **Plate** or **Sheet Metal**, a brighter buzz.

## Tips

- Try the real mains frequencies, 50 or 60 Hz, for a convincing electrical hum.
- **Voltage Sag** is the control for grit.

## Presets to try

- **Drones → Smokestack Hum**
- **Drones → Cog Hum Drone**
- **Experimental → Electromagnetic Hum**: one preset on each of the nine objects.
