A felt-covered mallet with a hard core: round and warm when played softly, with a brighter click when the felt is squeezed flat. That two-layer head is why velocity matters so much here: soft notes only touch felt, hard notes reach the core. It's the reason this mallet sounds so alive under your fingers.

**Try it:** load **Bass → Felt Cable Upright** and play soft notes, then hard ones: the hard notes reach the mallet's core and get brighter.

## What it is

A mallet head covered in felt. On a gentle hit only the soft felt touches the object, so the contact is long and soft. On a hard hit the felt is squeezed until the hard core under it takes over, and the contact gets stiffer and shorter. The mallet bounces off on its own; the object then rings freely.

## How it sounds

The roundest of the strikes. It brings out an object's low modes and keeps the high ones quiet, which suits basses, bells and gongs. Velocity changes the tone as well as the level, because hard hits reach the core.

## Controls

<ModelControls exciter="Felt Mallet" />

- **Mallet Mass:** heavier mallets stay in contact longer and bring out the low modes; they also hit louder.
- **Felt Softness:** higher gives a rounder, darker strike.
- **Core Hardness:** higher adds a brighter click to loud hits, once the core takes over.
- **Compression Curve:** how suddenly the core takes over as the felt is squeezed. Lower brings it in sooner, for a brighter strike.

## Pairings

Felt Mallet is the bank's bass exciter: it plays six of the twelve basses, on **Taut Cable**, **Pipe**, **Plate**, **Tank**, **I-Beam** and **Coil Spring**. On **Plate** or **Industrial Cog** higher up it gives bells and gongs.

## Tips

- Play with velocity: soft notes stay round and hard notes get a click, so a wide velocity range sounds alive.
- For a darker bass, raise **Felt Softness** and lower **Core Hardness**.
- Every factory drum kit uses Felt Mallet pads.
- If a bass gets lost in the mix, don't reach for the EQ first: lower **Felt Softness** a little so the hits carry more attack.
