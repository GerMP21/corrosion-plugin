A hand slapping the metal: the softest, most physical of the struck exciters, from a dull palm thud to a bright finger slap.

**Try it:** load **Bass → Palm Pipe Thump** and play low notes, then raise **Mute Decay** and play again: the hand rests on the pipe and chokes the ring.

## What it is

A fleshy hand hits the object and pushes back like a spring with damping: the flesh gives, then throws the hand off the metal. The hand can stay resting on the object after the hit, which mutes it the way a palm on a drum does. Its weight, the flesh's stiffness and damping, and how long it rests decide whether the strike reads as a slap, a thud or a muted knock.

## How it sounds

Soft-edged and warm, with less high-frequency click than a mallet or a stick. On a pipe or a tank it gives a deep, hand-drum thump; on a cable, a slapped bass; on a beam, a dull knock. It's the default exciter, so it's what you hear when you open Corrosion.

## Controls

<ModelControls exciter="Hand Strike" />

- **Hand Mass:** heavier hands hit louder and brighter.
- **Flesh Stiffness:** higher throws the hand back off the metal sooner, for a shorter, lighter slap.
- **Flesh Damping:** higher pushes back harder while the flesh moves: a louder, brighter hit.
- **Mute Decay:** how long the hand rests on the object after the strike. Higher keeps it there longer, so the ring is muted sooner and the note is quieter.

Like every struck exciter it uses the AD envelope, and **Speed** and **Roughness** don't affect it.

## Pairings

Hollow and low objects bring out the hand's warmth: **Pipe**, **Tank** and **I-Beam** give kick-like thumps, **Taut Cable** a slap bass. On bright objects like **Plate** or **Sheet Metal** it gives a soft, gong-like knock.

## Tips

- For a muted, percussive note, raise **Mute Decay**; for an open ring, lower it.
- **Hand Mass** is the quickest way to make the hit heavier.
- Six of the factory drum kits use Hand Strike pads for kicks and low toms.

## Presets to try

- **Bass → Palm Pipe Thump**
- **Bass → Hand Slap Cable**
- **Percussion → Beam Kick Thud**
- **Experimental → Hand Strike**: one preset on each of the nine objects.
