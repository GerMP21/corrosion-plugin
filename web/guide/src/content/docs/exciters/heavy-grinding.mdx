A grinder pressed against a rough surface: a dense roar of friction and tearing grit.

**Try it:** load **Drones → Grinding Beam**, hold a note and raise **Surface Grit**.

## What it is

A heavy tool scrapes across rough material. A steady friction force drags on the object, and on top of it the surface's roughness tears and catches, adding filtered noise. Pressure decides how much the tearing bites.

## How it sounds

Rough, dense and industrial: grinding, sanding, abrasive drones. At low settings it's a breathy rub; turned up, a roar.

## Controls

<ModelControls exciter="Heavy Grinding" />

- **Grind Speed:** the speed of the grinding wheel. Higher gives a brighter, denser roar.
- **Grind Pressure:** how hard the grinder presses. Higher is louder and more aggressive.
- **Surface Grit:** the coarseness of the surface. Higher adds noise and crackle; at 0 there's almost no tearing.
- **Grit Color:** the tone of the grit noise. Higher is brighter.

**Speed** scales **Grind Speed** and **Roughness** scales **Surface Grit**.

## Pairings

On **I-Beam** it gives a deep grinding drone; on **Plate** or **Sheet Metal**, a bright rasp; on **Tank**, a hollow rumble.

## Tips

- **Surface Grit** and **Grit Color** together set how noisy and how bright the grinding is.
- Loop the MSEG ([loop modes](/wiki/exciters/envelopes/#loop-modes)) for a pulsing machine.

## Presets to try

- **Drones → Grinding Beam**
- **Experimental → Heavy Grinding**: one preset on each of the nine objects.
