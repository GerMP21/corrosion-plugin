The exciter is what plays the object: a hand, a mallet, a bow, a jet of air. It decides how energy goes in, so it shapes the attack, the sustain and much of the character before the object adds its pitch and ring.

**Try it:** play a note, then change **MODEL TYPE** in the **EXCITER** column and play the same note again. The object, its pitch and the output chain stay the same; only the way it's played changes.

Corrosion has <Data count="exciters" /> exciters. Each one is a small physical model that pushes on the object every sample and feels the object push back, so the same exciter sounds different on a pipe than on a plate.

## Families

The exciters come in three families. The family decides which force envelope the exciter gets, and so which envelope controls the panel shows.

- **Struck** exciters hit the object once per note: Hand Strike, Felt Mallet, Hard Mallet, Drumstick, Wire Brush, Metal Pipe and Metal Chain. Their force follows a short **AD envelope** that starts on its own and doesn't wait for the key to be released, so a struck note rings out like a real strike.
- **Friction** exciters rub, scrape or pull for as long as the key is held: Bow, Stiff Point Scrape, Heavy Grinding, Corrugated Drag and Tension Rise. Their force follows the six-stage **MSEG**, which can loop, and the [gesture modifiers](/wiki/exciters/gesture-modifiers/) act on it.
- **Specialty** exciters are driven by something other than a hand or a tool: Pneumatic Jet, Electromagnetic Hum, Tension Snap and Particle Rain. Their force follows a classic **ADSR**.

See [Force envelope and MSEG](/wiki/exciters/envelopes/) for the three envelopes, and [Common controls](/wiki/exciters/common-controls/) for **Pressure**, **Speed** and **Roughness**, which every exciter has.

## Comparison

| Exciter | Family | What it is | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Hand Strike](/wiki/exciters/hand-strike/) | Struck | A palm or fingers slapping the metal | Thuds, slaps, muted hits |
| [Felt Mallet](/wiki/exciters/felt-mallet/) | Struck | A felt-covered mallet with a hard core | Round basses, bells, gongs |
| [Hard Mallet](/wiki/exciters/hard-mallet/) | Struck | A rigid mallet thrown at the surface | Clangs, plucks, bright hits |
| [Drumstick](/wiki/exciters/drumstick/) | Struck | A light stick that bounces back | Ticks, snares, cymbals |
| [Wire Brush](/wiki/exciters/wire-brush/) | Struck | A cloud of wires landing over a few milliseconds | Swishes, brushed hats |
| [Metal Pipe](/wiki/exciters/metal-pipe/) | Struck | A pipe that rings while it strikes | Anvils, metallic boings |
| [Metal Chain](/wiki/exciters/metal-chain/) | Struck | A chain dropped on the object | Rattles, clatter |
| [Bow](/wiki/exciters/bow/) | Friction | A rosined bow drawn across the object | Pads, strings, drones |
| [Stiff Point Scrape](/wiki/exciters/stiff-point-scrape/) | Friction | A stiff point that catches and chatters | Squeals, scrapes |
| [Heavy Grinding](/wiki/exciters/heavy-grinding/) | Friction | A grinder tearing at a rough surface | Roars, industrial noise |
| [Corrugated Drag](/wiki/exciters/corrugated-drag/) | Friction | A tool dragged over ridges | Rhythmic ticking, conveyors |
| [Tension Rise](/wiki/exciters/tension-rise/) | Friction | Stress that builds up and slips | Creaks, groans |
| [Pneumatic Jet](/wiki/exciters/pneumatic-jet/) | Specialty | A jet of air across an opening | Flutes, wind, breath |
| [Electromagnetic Hum](/wiki/exciters/electromagnetic-hum/) | Specialty | A coil driven by the mains | Hums, buzzes |
| [Tension Snap](/wiki/exciters/tension-snap/) | Specialty | A hook that pulls and lets go | Snaps, plucked basses |
| [Particle Rain](/wiki/exciters/particle-rain/) | Specialty | Many small particles falling | Rain, sand, granular beds |
