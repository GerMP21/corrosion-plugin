A stream of tiny particles falls on the object, each one a small impact: rain on a tin roof, sand on a sheet, gravel in a drum. How many fall, how heavy they are and how much their weight varies set the texture.

**Try it:** load **Drones → Particle Rain Tank**, hold a note and sweep **Flow Rate**.

## What it is

A stream of tiny particles falls on the object, each one a small impact. How many fall, how heavy they are and how much their weight varies set the texture.

## How it sounds

Granular and pattering: rain on a roof, sand, gravel, a shaker held still.

## Controls

<ModelControls exciter="Particle Rain" />

- **Flow Rate:** how many particles fall per second. Higher gives a denser rain.
- **Particle Mass:** the weight of each particle. Heavier particles hit harder.
- **Mass Variance:** the variation in particle weight. Higher sounds more uneven; at 0 every particle is the same and the rain is quiet.

**Speed** scales **Flow Rate** and **Roughness** scales **Mass Variance**.

## Pairings

On **Tank** it sounds like rain on a tank; on **Plate** or **Sheet Metal**, like rain on a roof.

## Tips

- A low **Flow Rate** gives separate drops; a high one, a steady wash.
- The Junk Percussion Kit has a Particle Rain pad.
- Rain on a bright, ringing object turns into a shimmering bed. Try it on **Plate** or **Sheet Metal** with some **Space**.

## Presets to try

- **Drones → Particle Rain Tank**
- **Experimental → Particle Rain**: one preset on each of the nine objects.
