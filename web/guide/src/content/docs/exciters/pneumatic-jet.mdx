A jet of air blown across the object: breathy, flute-like tones and wind.

**Try it:** load **Lead → Jet Pipe Flute** and play a melody, then raise **Turbulence Chaos** for more breath.

## What it is

An air jet drives the object with turbulent flow, like breath across a bottle or a flute. The jet's force grows with the air pressure and levels off at high pressure, so loud notes keep sounding.

## How it sounds

Breathy and soft: flutes, wind, breath and steam. On a pipe it's the most instrument-like of the specialty exciters.

## Controls

<ModelControls exciter="Pneumatic Jet" />

- **Air Pressure:** the pressure of the jet. Higher is louder and more turbulent; at its minimum the jet barely moves the object.
- **Nozzle Width:** the width of the nozzle. Higher gives a broader, louder hiss.
- **Turbulence Chaos:** the instability of the airflow. Higher sounds rougher and more chaotic.

**Roughness** scales **Turbulence Chaos**; **Speed** doesn't affect it.

## Pairings

**Pipe** is its natural partner, for flutes and wind drones; **Tank** gives a hollow breath.

## Tips

- The ADSR shapes the breath: a slower **Attack** for a gentle onset.
- For wind rather than a tone, raise **Turbulence Chaos** and play a big object.

## Presets to try

- **Lead → Jet Pipe Flute**
- **Drones → Pipe Wind Drone**
- **Drones → Tank Breath**
- **Experimental → Pneumatic Jet**: one preset on each of the nine objects.
