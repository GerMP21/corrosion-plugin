A stiff point dragged across the surface: it catches, bends and lets go, over and over, for squeals and scraping chatter. Think of a nail on a sheet of steel, or a chair dragged across a factory floor. Use it with care, and enjoy it when you do.

**Try it:** load **Drones → Cable Scrape Drone**, hold a note and sweep **Scrape Speed**.

## What it is

A rigid point, like a nail or a blade edge, presses into a moving surface. It catches, bends like a stiff spring, and slips free; the point's own spring rings each time it lets go, and that chatter is the force that drives the object.

## How it sounds

Squeaky, scratchy and tense: squeals, scrapes and nails on metal. It's harsher than the bow and less noisy than grinding.

## Controls

<ModelControls exciter="Stiff Point Scrape" />

- **Scrape Speed:** how fast the point moves along the surface. Higher raises the chatter's pitch and density.
- **Point Pressure:** how hard the point presses in. Higher is louder and harsher.
- **Chatter Pitch:** the tuning of the point's chatter, which colors the scrape. Higher sounds darker and softer.
- **Chatter Damping:** how quickly each chatter burst dies away. Higher sounds drier and tighter.

**Speed** scales **Scrape Speed**; **Roughness** doesn't affect it.

## Pairings

On **Taut Cable** it gives a scraped drone; on **Plate** or **Sheet Metal**, squeals. Low **Scrape Speed** on a big object gives slow creaks.

## Tips

- Use **Point Pressure** for intensity and **Chatter Damping** for how dry it sounds.
- **Release snap:** like the bow, a scrape can leave the object out of its rest position, and letting go quickly makes it spring back louder. Raise the MSEG **Release** to half a second or more to tame it.
- Squeals are loud in the upper mids. A little **Cutoff** in the Filter Bank keeps them exciting without making listeners wince.

## Presets to try

- **Drones → Cable Scrape Drone**
- **Experimental → Stiff Point Scrape**: one preset on each of the nine objects.
