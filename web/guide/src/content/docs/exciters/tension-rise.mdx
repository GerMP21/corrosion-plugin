Stress that builds up in the metal until it slips, then builds up again: creaks and groans. It's the sound of a ship's hull in a swell or a beam taking a load, and it's often more unsettling than any scream.

**Try it:** load **Drones → Plate Tension Swell**, hold a note and raise **Slip Stochasticity**.

## What it is

Force accumulates slowly, like a beam taking load or a cable being pulled, until it passes a threshold and the material slips with a sudden release. Then it starts building again. Each slip is a creak.

## How it sounds

Creaking, groaning and tense: ship hulls, loaded beams, doors. It can be a steady series of creaks or a random crackle.

## Controls

<ModelControls exciter="Tension Rise" />

- **Pull Speed:** how fast the tension builds. Higher makes the creaks come faster.
- **Break Threshold:** the tension at which the material slips. Higher builds more tension before each creak.
- **Slip Stochasticity:** the randomness of the slips. Higher sounds more irregular.
- **Creak Sharpness:** the sharpness of each creak. Higher sounds brighter and more percussive.

**Speed** scales **Pull Speed** and **Roughness** scales **Slip Stochasticity**.

## Pairings

On **Plate** it gives a tense swell; on **I-Beam** or **Tank**, deep structural groans; on **Taut Cable**, a strained cable.

## Tips

- Low **Pull Speed** with a high **Break Threshold** gives slow, heavy creaks.
- High **Slip Stochasticity** turns the creaks into crackle.
- Creaks are most convincing on large, low objects. Try a long **I-Beam** or a big **Tank** before anything small and bright.

## Presets to try

- **Drones → Plate Tension Swell**
- **Experimental → Tension Rise**: one preset on each of the nine objects.
