In ten minutes you'll change every part of a sound: what plays it, what it is, and what happens to it afterwards.

**Try it:** follow along in the plugin or in the [web app](https://corrosion-vst.org/play/), playing a note after each step. Hover any ⓘ badge on the way to see what a control does.

## Start from a preset

Open the preset menu in the **PRESET BANK** box and choose **Lead → Driven Pipe Lead**. Play a few notes around middle C. It's a drumstick hitting a pipe, with a little help from the output chain.

## Change the exciter

In the **EXCITER** column, open **MODEL TYPE** and choose **Bow**. Play and hold a note: the same pipe now sings, swells and fades, and when you let go it rings out as the bow leaves it.

The controls under the model changed too. Each exciter has its own panel (for the bow: Bow Pressure, Bow Speed, Rosin Grip and Slip Curve), and friction exciters get a six-stage force envelope instead of a simple attack and release. Raise **Bow Pressure** and play again: the tone gets grittier, then starts to scratch.

Go back to **Drumstick** before the next step, or keep the bow if you like it.

## Change the object

In the **RESONATOR** column, open **MATERIAL** and choose **Taut Cable**. Same exciter, different metal: a cable's partials bend down as it rings, a pipe's don't.

- The first control of each object sets its size, under a name that fits it. For the cable it's **Cable Length**. Lengthen it and play: the pitch drops.
- Under **Environmental Exposure**, raise **Rust**: the high partials dull and die sooner while the low thud keeps ringing. **Damage** cracks the object into beating pairs of partials.

## Shape the output

- Turn the four **Macros** at the top. Each moves a group of controls at once: **MASS** makes the sound heavier and lower, **CORROSION** more rusted and damped, **VIOLENCE** dirtier and more chaotic, **BRIGHTNESS** brighter.
- In **PROCESSING**, lower the Filter Bank's **CUTOFF** to darken everything, then add some **DRIVE AMT** in the Overdrive Unit.
- Under **Space**, set **MODE** to **Spring** and raise **AMOUNT** to put the sound in a spring tank.

## Save your sound

1. Type a name in the **Preset name** field.
2. Choose where it goes with the menu on its left (User, or a folder you made inside it).
3. Click **Save**. Your preset appears under User in the preset menu.

Factory presets are never overwritten. If you want variations to explore, set the menu next to **Randomize** to **Safe** and click **Randomize**: it draws a new sound within safe limits. [Randomizer](/wiki/randomizer/) explains the other modes.
