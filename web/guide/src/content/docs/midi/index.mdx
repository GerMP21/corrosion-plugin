Corrosion is played with MIDI: notes, of course, but also velocity, pitch bend, pressure, the mod wheel and the sustain pedal. Physical models love expressive playing, because every one of these reaches the physics, not just the volume.

**Try it:** load **Lead → Pipe Bow Lead** and play the same note softly, then hard. The hard note isn't only louder; it bites differently.

## Velocity and pitch bend

**Velocity** is how hard the exciter hits, pushes or blows. A harder hit drives the object further, which brings out more of its upper partials, so loud notes sound brighter, the way real metal does. On the friction exciters, the [gesture modifiers](/wiki/exciters/gesture-modifiers/) decide how much velocity also changes the envelope's level and timing.

**Pitch bend** retunes every sounding note, by up to 2 semitones either way. It moves the object's modes smoothly rather than restarting them, so a bend on a ringing note glides like a real one.

**Pressure** (channel aftertouch, or polyphonic aftertouch per note) and the **mod wheel** (CC1) both swell the voices on held notes: pressure by up to about 3.5 dB, the mod wheel by about 2 dB. Pressure is per note when your keyboard sends polyphonic aftertouch; otherwise it's shared by all notes.

Other controllers are ignored for now. To control a knob from your hardware, use your host's MIDI learn or automation.

## Sustain pedal

The sustain pedal (CC64) works like a piano's: notes you release while it's down keep going until you lift it. A note you play again while it's held by the pedal replaces the held one, so repeated notes don't pile up.

If you play more than 16 notes, Corrosion takes over the quietest voice that's already fading, then a pedal-held one, and only then a note you're still holding.

## Panic and all notes off

- **All Notes Off** (CC123) releases every held key. A sustain pedal that's down still holds them.
- **All Sound Off** (CC120) fades everything to silence in 5 ms, reverb and echo tails included. This is the "panic" message many hosts send when you stop or hit their panic button.
- **Reset All Controllers** (CC121) lifts the sustain pedal and puts the mod wheel, pitch bend and pressure back to rest.
