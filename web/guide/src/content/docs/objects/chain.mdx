A hanging chain: clustered, unstable modes and a short ring, for clanks, rattles and drags.

**Try it:** load **Percussion → Chain Mallet Clank**, then raise **Instability** between notes.

## What it is

A chain of linked pieces. Each link rings, and the links shift against each other, so the modes are clustered and slightly unstable rather than a clean series.

## How it sounds

Clanky, short and metallic. It decays on its own, so strikes stop by themselves; dragged or snapped, it gives rattling textures. It's inharmonic: tune it by ear.

## Controls

<ModelControls object="Chain" />

- **Chain Size:** the size. Larger chains sound lower.
- **Link Mass:** the weight of each link. Heavier links sound lower and heavier.
- **Instability:** how much the links shift against each other. Higher sounds looser and more chaotic.
- **Friction Decay:** the decay time of the friction envelope. Higher fades the friction slower. It acts on held notes.

## Pairings

Hard Mallet gives clanks; Metal Chain, a chain on a chain; Corrugated Drag, a dragged-chain drone; Tension Snap, a metallic snap.

## Tips

- A chain is one of the objects to pick when a strike must decay on its own.
- Pair it with a bright exciter for clank, a soft one for a dull knock.

## Presets to try

- **Percussion → Chain Mallet Clank**
- **Percussion → Chain Rattle Hit**
- **Percussion → Chain Snap**
- **Drones → Chain Drag Drone**
- **Experimental → *exciter* → *exciter* · Chain**: each of the 16 exciters has one preset on this object.
