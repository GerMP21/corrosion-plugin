A steel girder: low, heavy and long-ringing, with shear modes that add inharmonic overtones. This is the sound of construction sites and foundries, where the whole building rings when something heavy lands. Few objects say "weight" as clearly.

**Try it:** load **Percussion → Beam Kick Thud**, then raise **Shear Density** between notes.

## What it is

A structural I-beam. It bends along its length with low modes, and its web shears with extra modes that don't fit a harmonic series.

## How it sounds

Heavy, dark and long. Struck, it gives thuds, kicks and anvils that ring for seconds; bowed or ground, deep drones and chorales.

## Controls

<ModelControls object="I-Beam" />

- **Beam Mass:** the size. Heavier beams sound lower. At 1 it plays a tritone under the key; 0.707 puts it on the key.
- **Shear Density:** the strength of the shear modes. Higher adds more inharmonic overtones.
- **Rigidity Damping:** damping of the beam's stiffness. Higher shortens the high modes. It acts on held notes.

## Pairings

Hand Strike gives a kick; Metal Pipe, an anvil; Felt Mallet and Hard Mallet, basses; Bow and Heavy Grinding, drones.

## Tips

- Set **Beam Mass** to 0.707 to play in tune.
- A beam rings for seconds: for a short hit, raise **Rigidity Damping** or add Rust or Sludge.
- For cinematic hits, add **BODY** and a **Factory** Space: a struck beam in a big hall is half a trailer already.
