A metal tube: close to harmonic, long-ringing and hollow, from deep basses to flutes.

**Try it:** load **Bass → Hollow Pipe Sub**, then change **Tube Diameter** between notes.

## What it is

A hollow tube that rings along its length. Its modes sit close to a harmonic series, so it plays clear pitches, and it adds one fixed mode between about 800 and 1030 Hz, set by its diameter, whatever note you play.

## How it sounds

Round, hollow and sustained. Struck, it gives basses and bells that ring for seconds; blown with Pneumatic Jet, a flute; bowed, a singing lead. The fixed upper mode gives it its metallic tube colour, and is why it isn't used for chords: on several notes at once the fixed mode doesn't move with the harmony.

## Controls

<ModelControls object="Pipe" />

- **Pipe Length:** the size. Longer pipes sound lower. At 1.025 the pipe plays the key; at 1 it's 42 cents sharp.
- **Tube Diameter:** changes the hollow tone and moves the fixed upper mode.
- **Sustain Time:** how long the friction envelope sustains in Drone and looped modes. It acts on held notes.

## Pairings

Felt Mallet and Hand Strike give basses; Pneumatic Jet, flutes and wind; Bow, leads; Electromagnetic Hum, a droning hum. A pipe rings for seconds, so a strike that must stop on its own needs Rust, Sludge or a short envelope.

## Tips

- Set **Pipe Length** to 1.025 to play in tune.
- For a shorter, drier hit, add **Rust** or **Sludge** ([Environmental Exposure](/wiki/objects/environmental-exposure/)).
