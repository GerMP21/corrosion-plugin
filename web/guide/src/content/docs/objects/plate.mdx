A flat metal plate: bell-like overtones that decay on their own, from snares to pads.

**Try it:** load **Lead → Felt Plate Bell**, then turn **Plate Aspect** up between notes.

## What it is

A rectangular plate vibrating across its surface. Its modes spread out unevenly, the way a bell's do; the ratio of its sides decides how they're spread, and its stiffness how bright it is.

## How it sounds

Bright, bell-like and clear. It rings for a medium time and decays on its own, so it suits strikes: snares, bells, struck leads. Bowed softly, it gives glassy pads.

## Controls

<ModelControls object="Plate" />

- **Plate Size:** the size. Larger plates sound lower. At 2 the plate plays the key; at 1 it's an octave up.
- **Plate Aspect:** the ratio of the plate's sides. It changes how the modes are spread, from even to clustered; toward the maximum the strike gets much quieter and brighter.
- **Plate Stiffness:** higher spreads the overtones and sounds brighter.

## Pairings

Drumstick and Wire Brush give snares and brushes; Felt Mallet, bells and a sub bass; Hard Mallet, struck leads; Bow, pads and strings; Tension Rise, a tense swell.

## Tips

- Set **Plate Size** to 2 to play in tune.
- A plate is the object the factory chord patches use for soft bowed strings: keep **BODY** at 0 so the partials stay clean.

## Presets to try
