A thin metal sheet: dense, shimmering modes that warp as it rings, from organs to clangs.

**Try it:** load **Chords → Sheet Bow Organ**, hold a chord, then raise **Metal Thinness** and play it again.

## What it is

A large, thin sheet. Its modes are dense, and as it vibrates it bends and buckles, warping the pitches of its modes while it rings.

## How it sounds

Shimmering, wide and metallic. Bowed, it gives organs, shimmers and glassy leads; struck, clangs and hi-hats. Its pitch follows its size more slowly than the other objects.

## Controls

<ModelControls object="Sheet Metal" />

- **Sheet Size:** the size. Larger sheets sound lower. At 3.49 it plays the key, 1.371 an octave above and 8.8 an octave below; at 1 it's 425 cents up.
- **Metal Thinness:** the thinness of the sheet. Higher bends and warps the sound more.
- **Edge Damping:** damping at the sheet's edges. Higher shortens the ring. It acts on held notes.

## Pairings

Bow gives organs, shimmers and bass; Hard Mallet, clangs and a tick hat.

## Tips

- Use 3.49, 1.371 or 8.8 for **Sheet Size** to stay in tune; doubling the size doesn't drop it an octave.
- Lower **Metal Thinness** for steadier chords.
