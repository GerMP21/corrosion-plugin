A big hollow tank: low, boomy and long, with an air cavity that resonates inside it. Knock on an empty oil drum and you'll hear both parts at once, the ring of the steel and the boom of the air inside. That's this object.

**Try it:** load **Bass → Tank Boom Bass**, then raise **Cavity Mix** between notes.

## What it is

A large metal tank. Its shell rings with low, widely spaced modes, and the air inside adds a resonance of its own, like blowing across a bottle.

## How it sounds

Deep, booming and hollow. Struck, it gives booms and deep hits; blown or rained on, breathy and pattering textures. It's the lowest of the objects at the same size.

## Controls

<ModelControls object="Tank" />

- **Tank Size:** the size. Larger tanks sound lower. At 1 it plays 19 semitones under the key; 0.333 puts it on the key.
- **Tank Volume:** the volume of the tank. Higher sounds lower and boomier.
- **Cavity Mix:** the level of the air cavity's resonance. Higher sounds hollower and darker.
- **Shell Decay:** how long the shell rings. Higher sustains longer. It acts on held notes.

## Pairings

Felt Mallet gives a boom bass; Pneumatic Jet, a breath; Particle Rain, rain on a tank. Every exciter's comparison clip in this Wiki plays a Tank.

## Tips

- Set **Tank Size** to 0.333 to play in tune.
- **Cavity Mix** is the control for how hollow it sounds.
- Tanks have a lot of low end. In a busy mix, pair one with a low **WIDTH** so the boom stays centred and solid.

## Presets to try

- **Bass → Tank Boom Bass**
- **Drones → Tank Breath**
- **Drones → Particle Rain Tank**
- **Experimental → *exciter* → *exciter* · Tank**: each of the 16 exciters has one preset on this object.
