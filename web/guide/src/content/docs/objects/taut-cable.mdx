A stretched steel cable: harmonic like a string, and its pitch bends down on loud notes.

**Try it:** load **Lead → Hard Cable Pluck** and play soft and hard notes: the hard ones start slightly flat and settle.

## What it is

A cable under tension, vibrating like a string. Its modes form a nearly harmonic series, so it plays clean pitches and chords. A loud note stretches it and lowers its tension for a moment, so the pitch drops and then rises back as the note fades.

## How it sounds

Clear, string-like and long. Plucked or struck, it gives basses and plucks; bowed, strings, cellos and pads. It's the object behind most of the factory chord patches.

## Controls

<ModelControls object="Taut Cable" />

- **Cable Length:** the size. Longer cables sound lower. At 1 the cable plays the key.
- **Braid Stiffness:** the stiffness of the braid. Higher stretches the partials apart, for a less harmonic, more metallic ring.
- **Tension Drop:** how much the pitch drops on loud notes. Higher bends loud notes down further.

## Pairings

Bow gives strings, pads and cello basses; Felt Mallet, an upright bass; Hard Mallet and Drumstick, plucks; Hand Strike, a slap bass; Tension Snap, a snapped bass; Stiff Point Scrape, a scraped drone.

## Tips

- For chords, keep **Braid Stiffness** low so the partials stay harmonic.
- **Tension Drop** at 0 keeps loud notes in tune; raise it for a twangy bend.
