The parameter reference is the encyclopedia to the tutorials in the rest of the Wiki: one card for every parameter Corrosion has, generated from the plugin itself, so it's always exactly in step with the version you're running.

**Try it:** open [Exciter parameters](/wiki/reference/exciter/) and find **Bow Pressure**. The card tells you its range, its default, and lets you hear its extremes.

:::note[A word about the clips]
**Each control's clips change that one control and nothing else, on the same plain patch. Heard like that, plenty of them sound alike: a little brighter, a little longer, a touch rougher. That's honest, and it's not where Corrosion lives. The character comes when controls meet: a heavy object under a light, fast exciter, Rust on a bowed Plate, a long Space behind a dull strike. To hear that, play the [recipes](/wiki/recipes/), each with its before and after, and browse the [factory presets](/wiki/presets/factory-bank/).**
:::

The parameters are grouped by panel, in the editor's order:

- [Header](/wiki/reference/header/): presets aside, everything in the top bar.
- [Macros](/wiki/reference/macros/): MASS, CORROSION, VIOLENCE and BRIGHTNESS.
- [Exciter](/wiki/reference/exciter/): the model, its controls and its envelopes.
- [Resonator](/wiki/reference/resonator/): the object, its geometry, exposure and linkage.
- [Processing](/wiki/reference/processing/): the output chain.
- [Kit pads](/wiki/reference/kit/): what a Kit pad stores, and what the kit shares.

## How to read a card

- **The title** is the parameter's name as your host shows it in automation lanes and parameter lists. It can differ from the label on the panel: the Filter Bank's **Cutoff** is **Filter Cutoff** here.
- **The code** under it is the parameter's ID, the name presets and projects store it under.
- **The first line** says what it does, the same text as the ⓘ badge in the editor.
- **Range** or **Options**: what values it takes. **Default**: where a new instance starts. **Unit**, when it has one.
- **Shown**: when the control only appears in some cases (a family of exciters, a Space mode).
- **Per model**: for the controls whose meaning changes with the exciter or object, one row per model with its own label, range and help.
- **Clips**: the parameter at its minimum, middle and maximum, each played on a reference patch, so you can hear what the range does before you touch it. A few parameters have no clips, with the reason given.

The cards say *what*; the rest of the Wiki says *why* and *how to use it*. When a card leaves you curious, follow the link to the panel's page.
